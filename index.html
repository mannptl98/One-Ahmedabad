About ONE AHMEDABAD

ONE AHMEDABAD is a proposed unified digital citizen services platform designed to make municipal services easier to discover, access and track.

Our Vision

To bring municipal services into one accessible digital platform, enabling citizens to discover services, submit requests, make payments, receive guidance and track progress through a unified digital experience.
